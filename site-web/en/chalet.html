The chalet

A chalet designed for large groups

Six bedrooms and 23 beds, generous shared spaces: a 45 m² dining room opening onto a 25 m² lounge, all overlooking an enclosed garden.

Chalet Bellevue stands in the Domaine Bellevue, 1 km from the centre of Bagnères-de-Luchon. The chalet is completely self-contained and sleeps up to 24 people — ideal for family reunions, youth groups, association seminars or holidays with friends.

  • Bedroom 1 3 bunk beds (6 places)
  • Bedroom 2 3 bunk beds (6 places)
  • Bedroom 3 2 bunk beds (4 places)
  • Bedroom 4 2 bunk beds + 2 single beds 90 cm (6 places)
  • Bedroom 5 2 single beds 90 cm (2 places)
  • Bedroom 6 1 double bed 140 cm (2 places)

In total, 22 single beds and 1 double bed, arranged to suit families as well as groups.

Chalet tour

Explore each part of the chalet in photos

Kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, comfort, outdoors: everything your group will find on site.

Equipped kitchen

The kitchen is fully equipped to cook for a large group: two fridges and a freezer, a 5-burner gas cooker with gas oven, a dishwasher and a microwave. Three raclette grills, two coffee makers, a kettle and a toaster complete the equipment. Meals are served in the 45 m² dining room, which opens onto a 25 m² living room.

Bedrooms & beds

The chalet has 6 bedrooms for 2 to 6 people, 23 beds in all (22 single beds and 1 double bed), in a mix of single beds and bunk beds. Each family or group can easily find its place. Bed linen is available as an option.

Bathrooms & toilets

Two shower rooms with four individual showers and five washbasins, plus three separate toilets: plenty of facilities, designed for a large group. A hairdryer is provided.

Comfort

TV and free WiFi, gas heating, ironing equipment. The chalet has a private washing machine and tumble dryer, in the laundry room reserved for your group.

Outdoors

The 1,500 m² enclosed garden is reserved entirely for your group. It includes a terrace, a covered dining area with a barbecue and garden furniture. The private car park takes 8 cars.

Good to know

The chalet is a Jeunesse et Sport approved establishment. Pets are welcome provided they are mentioned when booking. Check-in is from 4 pm and check-out before 10 am. Full booking terms: see the Rates & terms.
