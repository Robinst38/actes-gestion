Group ski holidays

Group ski holidays in Luchon: Superbagnères and Peyragudes within reach

A chalet sleeping 24, 1 km from Luchon town centre, to head off skiing at Luchon-Superbagnères or Peyragudes and get together around a big table.

The resorts

Two resorts nearby

From the chalet, allow about 25 minutes to Superbagnères and 20 minutes to Peyragudes.

Luchon-Superbagnères

About 25 min from the chalet (18 km). The Luchon gondola takes 8 minutes to the top.

Peyragudes

About 25 min from the chalet.

Luchon thermal baths

To relax after a day on the slopes: the thermal baths are about 1 km from the chalet.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

How far are the ski resorts?

Luchon-Superbagnères is about 25 minutes from the chalet (18 km), with an 8-minute gondola ride from Luchon. Peyragudes is about 25 minutes away.

How many people can stay for a ski trip?

The chalet sleeps up to 24 people in 6 bedrooms. It is entirely self-contained.

Can we wash or dry clothes on site?

A private washing machine and tumble dryer are available to guests.

Does the chalet also host children’s groups?

Yes: it is a Jeunesse et Sport approved establishment. See our summer camps and children’s stays page.

What are the booking conditions?

Minimum stay of 2 nights, check-in from 4 pm, check-out before 10 am. 25% deposit when booking, together with the €30 annual membership of the A.C.T.E.S association; balance due one month before your stay.

How do we get a quote?

Via the form on the Groups page. Your request is confirmed within 48 hours.
