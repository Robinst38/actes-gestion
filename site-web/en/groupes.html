Groups & events

A group chalet in Luchon, all to yourselves

24 beds, 6 bedrooms, a 45 m² dining room and an enclosed 1,500 m² garden: Chalet Bellevue is made for bringing people together.

Who is it for?

Four ways to enjoy the chalet

The chalet is completely self-contained: your group has it all to itself, from the garden to the kitchen.

Family reunions

Everyone under one roof, from grandparents to grandchildren.

  • 24 beds, including a double bed and bunk-bed rooms
  • 45 m² dining room opening onto a 25 m² lounge
  • Enclosed 1,500 m² garden for the children
  • Covered dining area, terrace and barbecue

Associations, clubs & youth groups

Simple, practical group accommodation close to the slopes and trails.

  • Approved by Jeunesse et Sport
  • 2 shower rooms with 4 individual showers, 5 washbasins, 3 separate toilets
  • Private washing machine and tumble dryer
  • Skiing, hiking and outdoor sports nearby

Seminars & teams

Work together by day, unwind together in the evening.

  • Large 45 m² dining room for your meetings
  • Free WiFi and TV
  • Private parking for 8 cars
  • Luchon town centre 1 km away

Birthdays & weekends with friends

One big table and a kitchen built for large groups.

  • 2 fridges, 1 freezer, 5-burner gas cooker
  • Dishwasher, microwave, 3 raclette grills
  • Terrace and garden furniture
  • Spa, casino and Allées d’Étigny close by
