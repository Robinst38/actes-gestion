Groups & events

A group chalet in Luchon, all to yourselves

24 beds, 6 bedrooms, a 45 m² dining room and an enclosed 1,500 m² garden: Chalet Bellevue is made for bringing people together.

Who is it for?

Four ways to enjoy the chalet

The chalet is completely self-contained: your group has it all to itself, from the garden to the kitchen.

Family reunions

Finally, everyone together, from grandparents to grandchildren.

  • Cousins running around the big garden while the parents have coffee on the terrace
  • One long table for everyone, with nobody stuck in another room
  • The barbecue going in the late afternoon
  • Family memories you will talk about for years

Associations, clubs & youth groups

The Pyrenees as your playground, right outside the door.

  • Skiing at Superbagnères in the morning, back in the warm for afternoon snacks
  • Hikes to Lac d'Oô and the peaks around Luchon
  • A Jeunesse et Sport approved chalet, all to your group
  • Evenings together, just the 24 of you

Seminars & teams

Leave the office behind, come back as a real team.

  • Quiet meetings in the heart of the Pyrenees
  • A break at the Luchon spa, 1 km away
  • Dinners all together, where people say what they never say in meetings
  • A place that is yours alone, from breakfast to bedtime

Birthdays & weekends with friends

The weekend everyone will still be talking about next year.

  • A raclette night for 24 around the big table
  • Drinks on the terrace in the sun
  • A day on the slopes, an evening at the casino or a stroll along the Allées d'Étigny
  • The whole chalet to yourselves: party without bothering anyone
