Booking

How to book

  1. Pick your dates on the availability calendar (in French): the price is calculated instantly.
  2. Send your request: it is confirmed by the association within 48 hours.
  3. Pay a 25% deposit plus the compulsory annual A.C.T.E.S membership (€30). The balance is due one month before your stay.

Payment by cheque, cash or bank transfer. Questions? Use the contact form.
