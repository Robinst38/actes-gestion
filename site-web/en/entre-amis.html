Holidays & weekends with friends

Holidays with friends in Luchon: a whole chalet for your crew

Up to 24 people under one roof, a big shared table, an enclosed garden and the Pyrenees on your doorstep: Chalet Bellevue is made for get-togethers.

Why Chalet Bellevue

Everyone together, everyone at home

The chalet is entirely self-contained: your group has it all to itself.

Sleeps 24, 6 bedrooms

Room for the whole crew, with bedrooms for 2 to 6 people.

A really big table

45 m² dining room and 25 m² living room. Equipped kitchen with 5-burner cooker, oven, dishwasher and 3 raclette grills.

Outdoors too

1,500 m² enclosed garden, terrace, garden furniture and barbecue.

Comfort

Free WiFi, TV, gas heating, private washing machine and tumble dryer, 4 individual showers.

Well located

Luchon town centre 1 km away, thermal baths, casino and Allées d’Étigny a short walk away, private parking for 8 cars.

Mountains within reach

Skiing at Luchon-Superbagnères and Peyragudes, hiking to Lac d’Oô and in the Lys valley.

Stay ideas

Skiing, hiking, relaxing: you choose

A few ideas to plan your holiday or weekend.

Ski weekend

Luchon-Superbagnères about 25 min away (18 km), Peyragudes about 25 min. More details on our group ski holidays page.

Summer and outdoors

Hiking, mountain biking, climbing, via ferrata, paragliding, canyoning, horse riding: see the Surroundings page.

Relaxing in Luchon

Luchon thermal baths about 1 km away, strolls around the spa town, the casino and the Allées d’Étigny.

Frequently asked questions

What people ask us most

How many people for a holiday with friends?

The chalet sleeps up to 24 people in 6 bedrooms. You have it to yourselves.

Can we celebrate a birthday or a reunion there?

The chalet is well suited to birthdays and weekends with friends: 45 m² dining room, equipped kitchen, terrace and barbecue. Booking conditions are detailed on the Rates & terms page.

Is the kitchen equipped for a large group?

Yes: 2 fridges, 1 freezer, 5-burner cooker and oven, dishwasher, microwave, 2 coffee makers, kettle, toaster and 3 raclette grills.

What is the minimum stay and what are the times?

Minimum stay of 2 nights, check-in from 4 pm, check-out before 10 am.

Do we need to join the association?

Yes: an annual membership of €30 to the A.C.T.E.S association is required. It is paid together with the 25% deposit when booking.

How do we book?

Check the calendar on the Booking page, or request a quote. Your request is confirmed by the association's board within 48 hours.
